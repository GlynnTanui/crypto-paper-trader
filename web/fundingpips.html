All candidates, including failures

16 preregistered attempts

Four families × two parameters × two risk budgets. Development-qualified candidates and ineligible diagnostic controls are different categories—not interchangeable “winners.” The final period never selects a best strategy.

Preregistered eligibility gates & selection order

Economic baseline training and validation must each be net positive with profit factor ≥1.10, at least 100 training and 40 validation trades, both validation calendar quarters profitable, positive double-cost validation, and conservative intrabar drawdown <5% in training and validation. A3 additionally rejects data-indeterminate primary development paths: a gap-free prefix is not full-period evidence.

Score = minimum annualized simple return across training, validation and double-cost validation, divided by maximum of 1 and their intrabar drawdown percentages. Eligible first, then descending score, then lexical candidate ID. The first two distinct families are locked before final execution; unfilled places become explicitly ineligible diagnostic controls.

Managed development runs are also published. A losing final or final PF <1.10 does not establish a credible usable strategy. No post-final tuning or challenge-pass-rate optimization.

Sequential challenge scenarios

60 / 120 calendar days

Dependent, overlapping scenario fractions—not probabilities. Weekly starts share market history. Neither all-start nor fully-observed fractions estimate a future pass probability. The 60/120-day horizons are analytical windows, not firm deadlines.

Phase 1 must finish net +$400, flat, with ≥3 entry trading days. Phase 2 begins on a subsequent UTC date with a fresh $5,000, and must finish net +$250 with ≥3 entry trading days. No reuse of Phase 1 dates, discretionary target flattening, or recovery after a terminal stop.

How to read the outcome counts and medians

Pass: both sequential targets met in the conditional proxy. Breach: a modeled firm-floor hit, not a verified broker breach. Internal stop: the strategy’s own risk halt, not a firm breach. Inactivity: 30 days without a completed trade. Pending: target not attained by a fully observed horizon. Censored: still unresolved when available final data ends. Data-indeterminate: an open position crosses unavailable bars; its unresolved path remains in the denominator, separate from every other outcome.

“Fully observed opportunities” means the entire requested horizon lies within the final data window; it does not guarantee uninterrupted candles or a determinate path. These are a subset of all starts, not additional trials, and still include data-indeterminate paths. Phase medians use attained targets only, so a Phase 1 median can include scenarios that never pass Phase 2. Null medians mean not attained, not zero days. Administrative waiting between stages is unknown and omitted.

What the proxy actually models

Standard 2-Step · $5,000

Sequential targets
8% / $400, then 5% / $250. Fresh $5,000 for each phase; ≥3 entry trading days each. Current new-purchase Standard, not legacy 10%, Pro or Zero. [S1]
Daily loss floor
5% below max(opening balance, opening equity). The baseline locks at 21:00 UTC (00:00 at fixed current UTC+3). Balance or conservative liquidation equity touching the floor triggers the proxy. Historical DST is unknown. [S1]
Overall loss floor
Static 10% loss: $4,500. A touch counts; this is not a trailing 10% allowance. [S1]
Inactivity
30 days since a completed trade; not merely since opening one. [S1]
Internal risk
1% daily stop and persistent 6% close-peak-to-adverse-equity drawdown halt for managed/challenge runs. Per-trade inclusive stop-risk budget: 0.10% or 0.25% × min(initial capital, current equity). Gaps can exceed planned losses.
Leverage & size
Evaluation 2×, Master sensitivity 1×, conditional on unverified crypto availability. At most 90% of net available leveraged buying power, accounting for opening liquidation costs. Unit quantities floor to 8 decimals—not verified broker lots.

Signals, execution & costs

One position
Long or short CFD-style proxy; one position total, BTC before ETH deterministically. No pyramiding or future-return-based asset choice.
Causal signals
Completed UTC 1-hour bars from four validated 15-minute bars. SMA-seeded EMA20/80, Wilder ATR14 and 336 complete warmup hours. Execute only at the immediately following 15-minute open—not at the signal close.
Day-only hours
Mon–Thu entries 06:00 ≤ UTC <16:00; Friday 06:00 ≤ UTC <14:00. No weekend entries. Flat by 20:00 UTC daily, 16:00 Friday. Assumed cutoffs, not verified platform sessions.
Conservative exits
Stop fixed from signal close; target from actual entry and stop distance. Skip gaps >1 signal ATR or a stop on the wrong side of entry. Stop before target if both touch; adverse opening gaps fill worse, favorable target gaps get no improvement. Time exits use known bar closes; terminal liquidation uses the final close.
Base costs, per side
4 bp commission + 2 bp half-spread + 1 bp slippage. Double and triple scale all three. The published 0.04% commission formula is interpreted per side conservatively; actual side convention, contracts, lot sizes, bid/ask spreads, swaps and inventory are unknown. Spot OHLC is not CFD bid/ask. [S5]
Swaps & drawdown
Zero swaps assumed because modeled exits precede assumed rollover—not verified swap avoidance. Intrabar DD is a conservative OHLC ordering bound, not observed tick chronology. A possible risk hit is not proof of an actual broker breach.

Momentum literature motivates a hypothesis. Monthly futures evidence does not prove these hourly crypto CFD rules are profitable. Pullback, reversion and session effects remain conjectures. [H1]

What is not verified: news, Master rules, payouts and access

No historical authoritative news or speech calendar is available. Evaluation routine holding is not a deliberate-news-trading permission. There are no event-targeted rules here, but there is no verified-news-compliance claim. For Master sensitivity, USD events are conservatively relevant to crypto: −5/+5 minutes around events, speeches −10 minutes to +10 minutes after their end, with a ≥5-hour prior-entry exception. These restrictions are not implemented without event data. [S2] [S3]

Evaluation permits weekends. Current Master weekend holding is temporarily prohibited since 29 Jan 2026, including crypto. The strategy’s weekday-only, early-Friday-flat assumption does not verify platform session mapping. Master and evaluation requirements are distinct. [S3] [S8]

No payout cycle is selected and no funded/payout compliance is claimed. Biweekly: 80% / 14 days; weekly: 60% / 7 days, generally 1% minimum. On-demand: 90%, 2% minimum and 35% best-day consistency. New monthly: 100%, 35% consistency, seven ≥0.5% profitable days and 1% idea-risk striking. These are conditional Master rules, not evaluation gates. [S7]

The current 8%-target $5,000 Master is normally exempt from size-based idea-risk striking except the monthly choice. Same-instrument/direction concurrent trades or losing reentries within 10 minutes form one idea. No $25,000+ profit-concentration rule or Zero largest-loss-day rule is imported into this Standard $5,000 scenario. [S4] [S9]

Owned, self-developed EA evidence is required; third-party EAs are limited to risk management. VPN/VPS account access is prohibited, and API/webhook permission is unverified. GitHub Actions must never connect this experiment to a FundingPips account. There are no funding, trading or login controls here. [S2] [S6]

Full published limitations

    Sample completeness and outage bias

    Inspect provenance ↗

    Coinbase Exchange public spot candles are the input, not FundingPips executions. Every economic result and cohort fraction is conditional on this incomplete exchange record and its execution assumptions. A3 retains activity before gaps and marks unresolved open-gap paths indeterminate. Warmup restarts reduce later opportunities; missing volatile intervals still limit any safety claim.

    Data coverage, UTC gap dates and provenance have not been loaded.

    Data-only amendments, frozen before performance

    A1: Missing 15-minute source candles may be reconstructed only from three complete, real 5-minute Coinbase observations. No interpolation or synthetic OHLC; recovered A1 bars are not excluded gaps.

    A2 · superseded audit history: Proposed excluding all entries on any UTC date missing a bar in either asset, including entries before the missing time. That is an ex-post completeness filter using future availability, not live-deployable foresight; excluding outage dates may remove volatile days and bias economic/compliance evidence. A2’s excludedUTCdates concept must not be confused with the current inventory of UTC gap dates.

    A3 · current primary method: Retain pre-gap entries. An unavailable execution bar cannot fill a new order. Align BTC/ETH on real timestamps; restart hourly indicators only after an observable gap, with 336 complete warmup hours. Flat gaps preserve known cash. An already-open position makes the run/cohort data-indeterminate: preserve its entry fee, unresolved position and last-known marked equity, never fabricate an exit or erase a trade.

    No whole-day-filtered performance is computed in this version. A2 remains visible as superseded history, not current methodology. Original dates, families, costs, ranking formula and horizons remain fixed; the original protocol is immutable and amendments are explicit. Prefix metrics from an indeterminate path are not full-period profitability evidence.
